Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about booking appointments and listing your shop.

How does booking an appointment work?

Select your State and Area, browse verified barbers or hair stylists, choose your hairstyle or grooming service, and pick an open time slot. You'll receive instant confirmation with zero waiting line.

Are the prices listed on barber profiles final?

Yes. All barbers and stylists provide upfront transparent pricing with estimated durations. There are no hidden fees or surprise walk-in price inflation.

How do barbers and stylists list their shop?

Barbers and salon owners can click “For Barbers & Stylists” to complete our free 3-step registration. You can set your weekly hours, upload lookbook photos, add services, and start taking bookings immediately.

Can I reschedule or cancel my booking?

Yes. You can manage or cancel your appointment directly from your client dashboard or message your barber directly through the in-app chat.
