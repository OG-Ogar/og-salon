Find and book a barber

Choose your state/FCT and optional LGA or area council, open a barber’s profile, and choose a service. Sign in as a client to select a date and an available time, then submit your appointment request. Check My Bookings for its status.

Messages

Open Messages from your home. Send a message or use Refresh conversation to see new replies.

Products and collection

Open Products from your client home, choose available items and review your cart before placing an order. Follow the collection and payment instructions shown with your order.

Account recovery

Use Forgot password on your role’s sign-in page. If recovery is unavailable or your email does not arrive, contact support.
